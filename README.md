# Market-E-Commerce-Analysis
## Executive Summary:
Stakeholders have requested a review of the total revenue generated by country and insights into how discounts affect total sales. Using SQL and Power BI, I pulled transaction history data from a SQL server, organized it to show only relevant data regarding revenue, discount values, and countries involved. I found that the US is by far the largest revenue source of the countries. 

## Business Probelm:
Discounts offer a trade off when it comes to revenue, as it lowers the potential amount the business can receive but usually garners more sales. This overview is dedicated to starting analysis on the question: what is the best methodology of delivering discounts such that sales will be increased the most by?

## Methodology:
1. Use SQL to extract, clean, and organize the data into a more usable format from the database
2. Produce a dashboard in Power BI that visualizes revenue by country, discount amount, and over time.

## Skills:
SQL: CTEs, Joins, format, custom columns

Power Bi: DAX, ETL, calculated columns, data visualization, data modeling

## Results:
I found that close to 40% of the total revenue was generated by products under a discount, with a proportionally equivalent distribution across countries.

## Next Steps:
1. Normalize discount data to see which discount band offered the highest increase in sales
2. Find an intersection of offering discounts and sales that will optimize profits
